04Why CarBuy

Know what to check before you drive.

These prototype workflows help surface details worth reviewing. A demo badge is never a substitute for inspecting the car and original documents yourself.

01

Papers comparison

The review workflow compares uploaded chassis and registration details with the listing and flags owner-name mismatches for a human decision.

02

Suspicious listing signals

Moderators can see duplicate chassis or photos, unusual prices, contact details in descriptions and sudden posting activity.

03

Dealer review

A dealer application records trade-licence details and supports a manual visit or video check before a badge is approved.

04

Price context

Compare asking prices for a model, year and location. The prototype does not claim to know a final sale price.

05

Buyer privacy

Contact details stay out of public listing data. The demo requires a verified buyer identity before a contact reveal.

06

Transfer guidance

Read the ownership-transfer steps and check current forms and fees on the official BRTA portal before paying.
