Safety centre

Protect yourself during every step of a car purchase.

Before you visit

Compare the price with similar cars. Be cautious of pressure to send a bKash or Nagad advance. Meet in a public place and bring someone you trust.

Inspect the car and papers

Match chassis and registration numbers on the vehicle against the original documents. Check tax token, fitness and ownership details with the appropriate BRTA process.

Common scams

Watch for copied photos, an implausibly low price, a seller who refuses an inspection, or a request to move communication and payment outside the platform. Report suspicious listings.

Ownership transfer

Check the current forms, documents and fees together on the official BRTA portal before a transfer.
